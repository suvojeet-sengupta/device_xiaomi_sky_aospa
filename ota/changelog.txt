Changes
- Kernel: Suvkernel 3.0, now on Linux 6.6.162 (Android 6.6 LTS) with every module rebuilt and signed for it
- New CPU scheduler (CASS): tasks go to the core with the most spare capacity, so the UI stays smooth on less power
- The CPUs idle at their lowest speed again and clock up only when there is work, with no spikes while the phone is idle
- Real-time tasks no longer hold a CPU at its top speed
- Touch and app launch boosts from the perf HAL reach the CPUs again
- Faster charging: no more fixed thermal charging steps, and mi_thermald no longer cuts the charge current. The phone charges at the full speed the charger firmware allows, with only its own temperature safety limits
- Short, sharp vibration for taps and clicks (a crisp tak instead of a buzz), stronger overall
- Better battery while scrolling: the CPUs are no longer held at 1.5 GHz for every scroll
- The GPU starts at a higher clock, so apps open and scroll faster
- Louder speaker
- LDAC for Bluetooth earphones that support it
- NFC uses the right chip configuration for your region again
- Fewer apps killed in the background: a stock Qualcomm script no longer races the sky memory tuning at boot and resets huge pages, swappiness and zram compression
- Music players and workout trackers no longer get killed while running: the low memory killer no longer treats a short memory stall as an emergency
- Faster, steadier internet: BBR and fq_codel, TCP tuned for phones, and bigger socket buffers for QUIC

OTA updates
- Phones on 20261025 or newer update through Settings > System > System update. Older builds flash this one from recovery once
